Finances v2 for iOS

When opening or leaving journals and transaction lists, the top navigation/status area should stay covered without flashing transaction text behind it.

First-level accounts now align with their category heading, with only true subaccounts indented. Chart visibility and each journal's account expansion choices persist across navigation and relaunch. Long account names stay on one line. With future transactions present, All and account registers should open near today even when charts are enabled. Future entries remain above and reachable by scrolling; the chart preference stays enabled until you turn it off.

If an account, currency, template or its journal is deleted while its editor is open, saving should show an error and preserve the deletion.

Swipe journals or accounts left: Delete should be red, while Rename/Edit stays blue. Settings action menus have larger tap targets and descriptive accessibility labels.

Sync now shows Uploading or Downloading in the journal footer and Cloud Sync screen. Upload progress counts completed changes; downloads show an activity bar and changes received because iCloud does not supply a download total. Close the Cloud Sync sheet with Done or by swiping down and confirm sync continues in the footer. Turning Cloud Sync off should cancel it; finishing or turning it off should hide the progress bar.

Please test the simplified All Transactions view and Templates customization: no batch Edit button, permanent delete/reorder controls, and + at the top right. Check left-swipe Duplicate/Delete, duplicate date choices, recurring deletion scopes, and right-swipe cleared status.

Check receipt previews, backup restore, and iPhone/Mac sync after offline changes. Receipt files should survive restores, interrupted uploads, and shared-file deletions. Check long repeating schedules and nested accounts.

With Password Lock enabled, leave an editor open, background and reopen the app, then unlock and confirm the unsaved draft remains. Ordinary syncing should not relock an active session.

Report issues through TestFlight feedback with the build number and steps to reproduce.
